Cues

Tilt

Buzzes when your head leans away from your neutral posture.

Acceleration

Buzzes in the direction you are being pushed or are speeding up, with gravity removed.

Up and down movement buzzes all motors

Turning speed

Buzzes on the right side while you turn right, left side while you turn left.

Turn ticks

A short tap on the side you are turning toward, once for every set number of degrees turned.

Setup

Neutral posture

Sit or stand upright and look straight ahead, then tap the button. Tilt is measured from this posture.

Motor positions

Tap Buzz to find each motor, then set where it sits on the head.

Board mounting

Swap left and right

Check on the live view: lean right and the dot should move right. If it moves left, turn on Swap.

Motor drive

Raise the lowest power until the weakest cue is just felt. Buzz a motor at low strength to check.

Device

Feedback on

Higher focus keeps a cue on the nearest motor. Lower focus blends it across neighbouring motors.

Buzz once when ready
Low battery: three buzzes every 20 s

Accelerometer calibration

Place the board flat on a table, still, then calibrate. Do this once, before setting your neutral posture.

Settings backup

Copy the settings as text to keep them, or paste saved text and load it.
